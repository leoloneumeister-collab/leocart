Play now Meet the racers

Hold the slide. Earn the boost.

Drift longer and the sparks climb through three colors. Each one pays out a bigger mini-turbo when you let go.

  1. Blue

    0.85 seconds into the slide. A short kick of 0.55 seconds.

  2. Orange

    1.8 seconds. Nearly a full second of boost.

  3. Purple

    2.9 seconds. One and a half seconds of speed, if you can hold the line.

  4. Release

    Let go and it all fires. The kart kicks forward and the view stretches.

Six racers. No best pick.

Every racer's four stats add up to 12, so Vex's top speed costs him in the corners and Mochi's grip costs her on the straights.

Three tracks, three moods.

Three laps each, with checkpoints so nobody can cheat a lap. Your best times are saved on your device.

Meadow Run

A sunny beginner loop. Wide corners, clear sight lines, and the place to learn how a drift feels.

Dune Canyon

Sunset desert with two shortcuts across the hairpins. A boost pad marks each entrance, the dirt costs you speed.

Neon District

A night city with staircase chicanes. The hardest track to keep clean, and the best one to drift.

Last place gets the better items.

Six items for everyone. Two comeback items only show up for the back of the pack, so a bad first lap is never the end of the race.

  • Bolt

    Throw it forward, or backward while holding brake. Bounces off walls.

  • Seeker

    A homing missile that hunts the racer ahead of you.

  • Turbo Cell

    One strong burst of speed.

  • Turbo Trio

    Three short bursts, spend them when you like.

  • Oil Slick

    Drops a slick behind you. Rivals spin out.

  • Aegis

    A shield that absorbs the next hit.

  • Comet

    Comeback item. Autopilot at huge speed, and nothing can hurt you.

  • Pulse

    Comeback item. Shocks every racer ahead of you.
