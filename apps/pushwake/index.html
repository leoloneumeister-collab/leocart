How it works, and its limits
  1. Set a time and tap Set alarm. Allow the camera when asked.
  2. Leave this page open, screen on, phone plugged in and volume up.
  3. When it rings, put the phone face up on the floor and do pushups with your chest over the screen. The front camera sees you getting closer and counts each rep.
  4. Try Practise pushups first. If counting is off, change the sensitivity.

This is a web page, not a native app. A locked phone, a closed tab or Silent mode on iPhone will stop it from ringing. Keep your normal alarm as a backup.

The camera feed never leaves your phone. Only the average brightness is used.
