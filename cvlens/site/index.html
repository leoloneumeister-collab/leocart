    How it works

    No upload form, no account.

    1. Add CV Lens to your AI

      One time, a minute. Steps for ChatGPT and Claude are below.

    2. Paste your CV

      Ask it to "visualize my CV". The AI reads your text and picks out roles, dates and the skills each role used.

    3. Get the picture

      A timeline and a skills chart appear in the chat. Save it as an image for your portfolio or applications.

    Add it to your AI

    CV Lens is a connector built on MCP, the open standard both apps use for add-ons.

    ChatGPT

    1. Open Settings, then Apps (or Connectors).
    2. Turn on developer mode and add a new app.
    3. Paste the connector address below.

    Claude

    1. Open Settings, then Connectors.
    2. Choose Add custom connector.
    3. Paste the connector address below.

    Menu names change as the apps update. Once CV Lens is approved in the official directories you will be able to add it from there with one click.

    Questions

    Is my CV stored anywhere?

    No. The text goes from your AI app to the CV Lens server, which draws the chart and answers. Nothing is saved or written to a log. Details are on the privacy page.

    How are skills measured?

    By time. For every role your CV lists a skill in, CV Lens adds up the months, without counting overlapping jobs twice. It does not guess a skill level, because a CV does not say how good you are, only where you used something.

    Can the AI get something wrong?

    Yes. Your AI reads the CV and fills in the dates and skills, so check the chart against your CV. The Table tab shows exactly what was drawn. If a date cannot be read, CV Lens says so instead of guessing.

    What does it cost?

    The chat tool is free. You pay for your own ChatGPT or Claude plan as usual.
