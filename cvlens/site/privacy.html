Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

CV Lens draws a chart from the CV details you give it and keeps nothing afterwards. There is no account, no tracking and no advertising.

The connector (ChatGPT and Claude)

  • What it receives. When you ask your AI to visualize a CV, the app sends the details it read from your CV (name, roles, employers, dates, skills) to the CV Lens server.
  • What it does with it. It checks the dates, adds up the years, and returns the data the chart is drawn from. The chart is drawn on your device, inside the chat.
  • What it keeps. Nothing. The request is not saved to a database, a file or a log. It lives in memory for the fraction of a second it takes to answer.
  • Rate limiting. To stop abuse, the server counts requests per network address in memory for one minute. These counts are not logged and are gone after a restart.
  • Hosting. The server runs with a hosting provider, which may keep ordinary infrastructure logs such as time and network address, and not the content of your requests.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.
  • The demo runs entirely in your browser. What you type into it never leaves your device.

Your AI app

ChatGPT and Claude have their own privacy policies, and they see everything you paste into them. CV Lens only sees what the AI sends to it for the chart.
